Action Verb Prompt Cards

Print these cards, cut along the dotted lines and use them for matching, sentence-building or partner practice. Learners can describe an action instead of moving. The cards are deliberately text-only and easy to adapt.

READ
a story
WRITE
a sentence
LISTEN
to a song
SPEAK
to a friend
DRAW
a picture
PLAY
a game

Three ways to use the cards

  1. Choose a card and complete “I can…” with the action.
  2. Read a sentence while your partner selects the matching card.
  3. Join two actions with “and”, then ask a partner what they can do.

Adaptation notes

Add your own drawings or openly licensed pictures. Use larger type where needed. Replace unfamiliar actions with tasks learners know. Allow extra thinking time before asking for a spoken response.
