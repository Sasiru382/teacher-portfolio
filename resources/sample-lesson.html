Action Verbs · Sample Lesson Plan

Level: upper primary / early secondary · Duration: 40 minutes
Topic: everyday actions · Resources: picture cards, board, worksheet

Learning objectives

  • Identify six everyday action verbs: read, write, listen, speak, draw, play.
  • Create a complete sentence using “I can…” and an action verb.
  • Listen to a partner’s sentence and identify the action described.

Lesson sequence

  1. Warm-up · 5 minutes: show or describe familiar activities. Ask what learners can already name.
  2. Explore · 8 minutes: match action cards to words. Model pronunciation and a sample sentence.
  3. Practise · 12 minutes: pairs take turns choosing a card and describing or miming the action. Offer a seated or spoken alternative.
  4. Create · 10 minutes: complete the worksheet, then share three original sentences with a partner.
  5. Reflect · 5 minutes: each learner records one new verb and one question on an exit ticket.

Support and extension

Provide a word bank, visual prompts, extra processing time and paired rehearsal. For extension, use “She can…” or join two actions with “and”. Do not require physical movement.

Assessment for learning

Listen for accurate verb choice and a complete sentence. Note one class-wide misconception. Give feedback on meaning before minor pronunciation corrections.

After-lesson note

Which learners could use the target language independently? What should the next lesson revisit?
