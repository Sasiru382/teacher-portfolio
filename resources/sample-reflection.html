Teaching Reflection Journal

Date: ______________   Lesson / topic: ___________________
Class code (no names): ______________

1. What happened?

Describe one specific classroom moment. What did learners do or say? Keep observations separate from assumptions.

2. What does the evidence suggest?

Which objective was met? What work, questions or participation patterns support that judgement? Whose needs were not yet met?

3. What did I learn?

Identify one strength, one difficulty and a possible explanation. Connect your reflection to mentor feedback where relevant.

4. What will I change?

Choose one practical adjustment for the next lesson. How will you know whether the adjustment helped?
