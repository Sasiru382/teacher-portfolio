Action Verbs · Learner Worksheet

Name or learner code: ___________________   Date: ______________

Today we are using action words to talk about things we can do. Use this word bank: read · write · listen · speak · draw · play.

1. Choose the best action word

  1. I __________ a story in my book.
  2. I __________ a picture with my pencil.
  3. I __________ to a song.
  4. I __________ a game with my friend.

2. Make it your own

Write three true sentences. Begin each sentence with “I can…”. You may use the word bank or a different action word.

3. Talk with a partner

Read one sentence to your partner. Ask: “What can you do?” Listen carefully and write one thing your partner can do.

Teacher’s answer guide (do not print with learners)
