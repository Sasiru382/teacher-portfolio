Before adding a document

  • Does the document support a specific, accurate statement?
  • Have names, signatures, contact details and learner identifiers been redacted?
  • Do you have permission from the copyright owner and school?
  • Is the file readable, dated and clearly titled?
  • Is it necessary to publish the full document, or would a redacted excerpt suffice?
